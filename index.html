Privacy Policy — Swipe Slither

Effective date: upon first publication on Google Play
Developer: Mahadi Hasan
Contact: mahadi8e@gmail.com

What this app does

Swipe Slither is a classic Snake arcade game for Android. You steer the snake, eat apples to score points, and track your progress in a profile.

Information we collect

We collect nothing. The app does not create an account, does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location, and does not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party SDK that collects data.

Information stored on your device

The following stays only on your phone and is never sent anywhere:

  • Your points balance, best score, and games played
  • Your profile name and username (which you can edit in My Profile)
  • Your purchase history inside the app

If you uninstall the app, this information is deleted with it. If you change phones, it does not carry over.

Payments

Point packs are sold through Google Play Billing. Payments are processed by Google; we do not see or store your card details. When a purchase succeeds, the points are credited to your balance on your device, and the order is recorded in the purchase history inside the app (on your device only).

Internet

The app requests internet access but, in the current version, sends no data to any server. All game data is stored on your device.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles data (for example, if online features are added later), this policy will be updated and the new version will be posted at this same address.
